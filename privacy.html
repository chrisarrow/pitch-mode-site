Privacy Policy

Pitch Mode · Effective October 10, 2026

Short version: Pitch Mode collects no data. Your decks, notes and pitch history never leave your iPhone.

What the app stores

The presentations you open, the slide images made from them, your talk tracks and tags, your settings and your pitch and rehearsal timings are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups the same way as other app data, under your control.

What the app collects

Nothing. Pitch Mode has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers. It does not connect to the internet: slides are drawn on the device, and web content inside a deck is blocked from loading.

Sharing

No information is shared with or sold to anyone. When you present, the slide image is sent only to the display you connect (over AirPlay or a cable).

Children

Pitch Mode collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a deck in the app to remove it and its history, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: pitch-mode@chrisarrow.com.
